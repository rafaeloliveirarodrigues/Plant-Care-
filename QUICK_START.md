# 🚀 Quick Start Guide

Get your household watering together in a few minutes.

**Open the app:** https://plant-care-ruddy.vercel.app/

## 1. Sign in

1. Enter your name and email address.
2. Click **Email me a sign-in link**.
3. Open the email and click the link. You're signed in, with no password needed.

Your name is what everyone else sees in the watering history.

## 2. Create or join a household

**If you're the first person:**
1. Under **Create a household**, enter a name (e.g., "Kitchen plants").
2. Click **Create household**. You'll start with five plants: Basil, Mint, Tomato, Succulent and Aloe Vera.
3. Share the **invite code** shown at the top with your flatmates, and only with them.

**If someone already created one:**
1. Ask them for the invite code.
2. Enter it under **Join a household** and click **Join household**.

Each person can belong to one household.

## 3. Water a plant

Click **💧 Water Plant** on a card after you water it. Everyone in the household sees the update straight away, on any device.

Check the badges to see what needs attention:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ All Good | Nothing to do |
| 📅 Water Tomorrow | Due tomorrow |
| ⏰ Water Today | Due today |
| ⚠️ Overdue | Past its watering date |
| Not watered yet | New plant with no watering logged |

## 4. Make it yours

- **⚙️ Manage plants:** add, edit or delete plants and set how often each needs water.
- **🔔 Notifications:** turn on hourly reminders while the app is open.
- **🌦️ Weather** (household creator only): set your city so outdoor and greenhouse plants get forecast-based watering suggestions.

## Tips

💡 **Add it to your home screen.** On your phone, use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" for an app-like shortcut.

💡 **Set the environment for each plant.** Mark plants on a balcony or in a garden as **Outdoor** so the weather guidance applies to them.

💡 **Keep invite codes private.** Anyone with the code can join your household and edit your plants.
